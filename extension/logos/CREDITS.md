# Company logo credits

Company Lens bundles logos locally to identify the companies named by its question dataset. Logo images never trigger runtime requests to third-party logo services. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; inclusion does not indicate sponsorship or endorsement.

## Simple Icons

Vector logo geometry and brand colors come from the official [Simple Icons repository](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), at the immutable revisions recorded in `catalog.json`. Current catalog matches are preferred; a pinned 11.15.0 catalog supplies brands absent from the current catalog. Only exact normalized names and the explicit reviewed aliases recorded in the catalog are matched.

The Simple Icons project is released under CC0-1.0. Its license and disclaimer are included beside this file. Individual icon license details, official source pages, branding guidelines when supplied by the upstream project, and the exact upstream asset URL are recorded per asset in `catalog.json`.

Packaged SVGs contain static geometry and a brand-color fill, with upstream presentation metadata omitted. No scripts, event handlers, external references, or embedded resources are present.

## Official company favicons

Other logo assets come from the companies' official websites and are used as identification marks. Each catalog entry records the website, public asset URL, retrieval time, original source checksum, and packaged checksum. Raster assets are decoded and saved as compact PNG files; accepted SVG favicons contain only static geometry. Branding rights remain with each owner.

Ambiguous company names, unavailable logos, and source-era names that redirect to another brand use the extension's initials fallback. Logos do not imply that the source dataset is complete or verified by a company.
